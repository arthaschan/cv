Project 03

mentalDistill Medical MCQ Knowledge Distillation Platform

A full applied AI path from research question and experiment design to validated outcomes.

Master's Thesis · Applied AI
89.10% best accuracy 88.67% three-seed mean 17 experiment modules 991-question test set
Fit

AI Product Manager / Applied AI / LLM Evaluation and Experiments / Technical Research

Problem

The task was to transfer teacher-model capability on medical multiple-choice questions into a more deployable student model without losing rigor.

What I did

I built the experiment platform independently, compared Choice-Head, multi-teacher, student scaling, and data repartitioning strategies, and produced the full report and figure set.

Result

The 14B student reached 89.10% on the 991-question test set with an 88.67% three-seed mean, and the result remained stable on the larger evaluation set.
