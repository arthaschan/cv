Project 03

mentalDistill Medical MCQ Knowledge Distillation Platform

A full applied AI research path from hypothesis and experiment design to result validation.

Master's Thesis · Applied AI
89.10% best accuracy 88.67% three-seed mean 17 experiment modules 991-question test set
Fit

AI Product Manager / Applied AI / LLM Evaluation and Experiments / Technical Research

S · Situation

The objective was to transfer the answering ability of large teacher models on medical multiple-choice questions into a more deployable student model, balancing effectiveness, cost, and reproducibility.

T · Target

The goal was not only higher accuracy, but also a clear understanding of which method works, why it works, and whether the result remains stable on a larger test set.

A · Role

Independently built the experiment platform, designed the modular experiment path, and produced code, reports, figures, and defense materials.

A · Methods

Baseline construction, Choice-Head distillation, multi-teacher comparison, 14B student scaling, full-data repartitioning, and heterogeneous-teacher distillation.

R · Result

The 14B student model reached 89.10% on the 991-question test set, with a three-seed mean of 88.67%, and the result remained stable on the larger evaluation set.
