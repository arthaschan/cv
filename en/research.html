Master's Thesis Research

Automatic Dental Multiple-Choice Question Answering via Knowledge Distillation

I built a reproducible CMExam medical MCQ experiment platform, used Choice-Head and related methods to push a 14B student model to 89.10%, and validated the result on a larger test set.

Research Summary

Research Overview

The goal was to transfer the medical QA capability of large teacher models into a more deployable student model. I built a modular research platform and systematically compared teacher quality, distillation methods, student scale, and data size, ultimately bringing the 14B student model to 89.10% on a 991-question test set.

Method

Four method steps worth keeping

Build a baseline first

Established a GT SFT baseline to ensure every later gain had a clear point of comparison.

Compare distillation paradigms

Systematically compared white-box KL, black-box Choice-Head, multi-teacher fusion, and chain-of-thought distillation.

Focus on Choice-Head

Distilled only the A/B/C/D/E answer distribution for five-option questions, grounding the method in task structure itself.

Expand evaluation last

Expanded the test set from 83 to 991 questions to confirm stability on a larger sample.

Findings

Three conclusions worth highlighting

Finding 1

Choice-Head is driven by task structure

Modules 02 / 13 / 15

Because it distills directly around the five answer options, it reduces implementation and deployment complexity while fitting the task better than heavier white-box methods.

Finding 2

More Stage 2 training is not always better for stronger students

Module 13

The 14B student peaked at 84.34% with single-stage distillation, and additional GT SFT could degrade performance, showing that method design must account for the student's own baseline.

Finding 3

A larger test set shows the student can stably outperform the teacher

Module 15

The 14B student achieved an 88.67% three-seed mean and a best score of 89.10%, exceeding the teacher's 87.18%, while the three-seed range remained only 0.70pp.
